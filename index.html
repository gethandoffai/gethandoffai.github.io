06Questions

Fair questions.

Do I need to be good with tech?

No. We build it and set it up with you. If you can send a text, you can use it.

Will it send things without asking me?

Only if you want it to. It starts by drafting for your OK. You decide what it can send on its own.

Do I really see it before I pay?

Yes. That's the whole point of the demo. We show it working on your own examples, then you decide.

What if I use ChatGPT, not Claude?

The Kit runs on Claude Pro. Managed doesn't need any AI account from you at all.

Is there a refund?

No refunds, because you see it work before you buy. After setup you get 30 days of adjustments to get it right.

Can I cancel Managed?

Anytime. It's month to month with no contract. Just give us 30 days notice. The setup fee isn't refunded.

Who's it for?

Owner-run businesses with 1 to 20 people. Salons, studios, clinics, shops, creators, consultants.
